6. What I'd do next

I'd turn this into a tracker. Every week it pulls the new reactions, adds anyone new, and flags when an account heats up, like a second person from the same company or a data quality owner reacting twice. That's when someone from Soda should reach out.

Past the reactions, I'd also track what these people watch and read around Soda, since a reaction only shows who stopped to click. Then I'd write for each type based on that. Data quality owners get regulation and stories from peers at companies like theirs. Platform builders get walkthroughs inside their own stack, and Soda's commit log already has plenty of those, like the one about SQL Server reporting the average of 0..9 as 4. The solo learners get the data contract explainers they already like.

Then the same funnel shows whether more data people showed up. Florent and Matthieu already told their story at your booth, so I'd start by asking them to tell it on LinkedIn too.

This page is the first run of it, and I'd like to keep running it for Soda.

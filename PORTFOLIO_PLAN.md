# Portfolio rebuild plan

Date: 2026-07-15

## The job of the site

The site has to make a short, unusual timeline feel credible. A recruiter or
founder should leave knowing what Sergey personally built, why he built it, how
it reached a real workflow, and where the evidence stops.

The portfolio is aimed first at Growth Engineer, GTM Engineer, and AI Product
Engineer roles. It should still feel like Sergey: direct, curious, fast, and
more interested in the working object than in polished personal branding.

## Core thesis

Sergey gets close to an unclear workflow, finds the part that needs to become
concrete, and builds it.

The opening proof is specific: he built a physician-discovery system for
FutureClinic during the hiring process. His current proof is Fyxed, where the
work spans sourced market research, GTM systems, and a PM-branded product test.

## Public structure

### Home

1. A direct opening built around the pre-hire FutureClinic story.
2. A compact current-role note for the active Fyxed contract.
3. Two case files, with FutureClinic first and Fyxed second.
4. A short creator-origin section that explains why video and audience judgment
   transfer into product work.
5. Resume, LinkedIn, GitHub, and email.

The home page does not contain a generic project grid, a metric strip, a tool
cloud, or a self-explanation of the portfolio strategy.

### FutureClinic case

This is the flagship case. It uses a five-scene case reel:

1. The proof built before the role: Derm Hunter.
2. The creator context that shaped the product decision.
3. The original end-to-end FutureClinic Creators implementation.
4. Personalized doctor previews and human-approved outreach.
5. The evidence boundary: live product continuity, founder validation, and the
   outcomes that remain unknown.

### Fyxed case

This is current work, written in present tense. It uses the same five-scene
reel:

1. Learning a gated property-management workflow.
2. Building the source-backed market and decision-maker layer.
3. Seeing repair funding recur in real conversations.
4. Turning an existing repair bill into a PM-branded owner choice and manual
   review flow.
5. The evidence boundary: current prototype and live-case learning, with no
   launch, approval, funding, or product-market-fit claim.

### Resume

The existing one-page resume remains a separate, conventional artifact. The
site links to it but does not copy its layout.

## The project-showcase device

Each case has a `Case reel` that works in two modes:

- Inline mode lets a visitor move through the scenes without leaving the page.
- Presentation mode opens the case as a clean full-screen sequence with arrow
  keys, visible progress, concise talking points, and no surrounding page
  chrome.

The reel makes the work easy to present in an interview. It also forces every
case to keep a clear sequence: event, interpretation, build, observed proof,
and boundary.

The FutureClinic video is an enhancement. If its transcript, pacing, privacy,
and playback checks pass, it will occupy the product-build scene. Until then,
that scene uses a complete interactive reconstruction.

## Visual system

The visual direction is a working studio file:

- warm paper and near-black ink;
- one high-visibility signal color;
- hard grid lines and flat surfaces;
- small monospaced labels for dates, roles, and evidence;
- large editorial type used sparingly;
- product screens that look like working objects;
- no gradients, glass cards, floating decoration, pill-heavy navigation, or
  fake dashboards.

The personality comes through the sequence and the details of the work. The
site does not announce a persona.

## Claim and privacy rules

- Public repository measurements remain clearly labeled as system-run facts.
- Private evidence can support a public paraphrase but never appears as a link,
  path, screenshot, contact record, or raw quote.
- FutureClinic product continuity does not imply current adoption or unchanged
  code lineage.
- Fyxed uses only aggregate data and fictional product reconstructions.
- Every Fyxed screenshot says it is a reconstruction based on an internal
  prototype.
- The active Fyxed role stays present tense and explicitly says `Contract`.
- Unsupported outcome, financing, customer, employment, and education claims
  stay out.

## Completion checks

- The site builds and every public route renders.
- The case reel works with buttons and a keyboard.
- Presentation mode has a clear close path and useful focus behavior.
- The home page and both cases remain complete without the video.
- No private source marker or blocked claim reaches rendered HTML.
- Legacy public URLs have safe redirects.
- The social preview matches the finished site.
- A private Sites deployment succeeds before any production-domain decision.

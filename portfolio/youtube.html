My YouTube channel

I took videos from the first idea through publication and built a channel with over 18 million lifetime views.

The idea had to work as a video

Before working on growth at startups, I built SergeCode, a Roblox YouTube channel. I handled the full video process myself. Choosing what to make, explaining it, and getting the final video published were all part of the same job.

That experience is a big part of how I think about growth. The way an idea is presented affects whether someone gives it their attention, and I had to follow that through in the finished work.

One game, 3.72 million views

In “I made a scary game on Roblox in 24 hours...”, building the game was also the story of the video. It reached 3.72 million views and brought in 59,330 subscribers in YouTube Studio's lifetime report.

Across the channel, that work reached more than 18 million lifetime views and an audience of around 200,000 subscribers. I now bring that experience into content products, technical explanations and the way I package an offer.

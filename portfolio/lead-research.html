Field notes / 03 — Research & automation

Prospect intelligence

I built Fyxed's prospect research system from scratch, turning company websites into specific reasons to start a conversation.

Start with the business problem

When I joined Fyxed, the prospecting system wasn't in place. I identified third-party residential property managers as the target, used NARPM membership as a starting point, and gathered company websites and contact information.

The useful question was what each company promised its owners. A manager that committed to a payout date, or carried a penalty for missing it, gave us a much more specific opening than a company name on a list.

Build the signal into the research

I built a workflow that read company websites for owner-payment timing and guarantees. It separated those promises from ordinary tenant rent collection and generic payment options. Each positive classification carried a source quote, so the team could see the reason behind the flag.

The run assessed 3,357 companies and surfaced 510 owner-payment signals. I also researched 121 PMI franchise domains in a separate pass, checking guarantees, pricing and the people behind each office.

A researched starting point for outreach

This gave the team a researched starting point for outreach: who the company served, what it promised owners, and a relevant angle for the message. I carried that research into list preparation and personalization, keeping the website evidence close to the account.
